Methods (draft)

Raw counts for primary human airway smooth muscle cells from four donors were downloaded from the Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO accession GSE52779). Cells were treated with 1 uM dexamethasone for 18 hours as described by Himes et al. (PLoS One 2014; doi:10.1371/journal.pone.0099652). Differential expression was tested with a model including donor and treatment.
